Frequently asked questions

How much does a house rewire cost in Leeds?

A full rewire of a three-bedroom house in Leeds typically costs £4,500 to £6,500 including certification, depending on access and the number of circuits.

How long does an EV charger installation take?

Most home EV charger installations take three to four hours. We'll survey your supply first, and you'll get your certificate the same day.
