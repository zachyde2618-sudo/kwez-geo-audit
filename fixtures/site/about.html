About Bright Spark Electrical

We are a family-run electrical contractor based in Leeds city centre. We have completed more than 1,200 domestic jobs since 2012 and are an NICEIC approved contractor, registered with TrustMark.

Meet the team

Jo Bloggs

Jo Bloggs, Founder & Director, founded Bright Spark in 2012 after eleven years as an approved electrician with a national contractor. Jo holds the City & Guilds 2391 inspection and testing qualification and the 18th Edition wiring regulations certificate, and has personally signed off more than 600 EICR certificates for Leeds landlords. Jo also trains apprentices at Leeds College of Building.

Sam Patel

Sam Patel is our lead EV charger installer. Sam is OZEV-approved, has fitted over 300 home chargers across West Yorkshire and specialises in tethered and smart chargers for terraced houses without driveways, working with the council on cable-channel permissions where needed.
